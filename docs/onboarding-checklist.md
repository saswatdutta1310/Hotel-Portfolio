# Onboarding checklist: what to collect from each hotel

- Written permission to use their name, photos and text
- Hotel name, tagline, short description (their own words)
- Full address, pincode and map location
- Phone, WhatsApp number, email
- Check-in and check-out times
- Room types: name, size, bed, max guests, amenities, starting price per night
- Amenities list, house rules, cancellation and ID rules
- Nearby places with distances, and how to reach from the station, airport, bus stand
- 10 to 20 good photos: exterior, lobby, each room type, bathrooms, surroundings
- Google Business Profile link and any review links
- Instagram and Facebook links
- Their own domain name, if they have one
